A simple learning loop

From a finished game to a clearer next move.

The platform adds a learning layer after the game is over, so players can focus on decisions they can improve.

01 / CONNECT↗

Choose your games

If access is approved, players will be able to authorize a connection and choose games they want to understand better.

02 / REVIEW⌕

Understand key moments

A post-game review walks through important decisions and compares the played move with stronger continuations.

03 / PRACTICE♘

Build a useful habit

Recurring patterns can guide focused chess study, helping players revisit ideas that matter in their own games.

Chess.com OAuth access request · intended use

To analyze games, with the player's permission.

OAuth would let users connect their Chess.com account so Inteligência no Xadrez can retrieve the games needed for post-game analysis. The purpose is educational: help players understand their decisions and identify what to study next.

Chess.com remains the place where users play. This independent platform does not host Chess.com games, submit moves, or compete with Chess.com's playing experience.

✓Access is requested to support analysis of a user's games.
✓No playing or move submission through the integration.
✓The player authorizes the connection through Chess.com.
